**Degeneration**:

Has my writing deteriorated? 

I’m reading some of my past works, essentially all the pieces from when I was in China the previous two summers (2024 and 2025), and they seem so much more cohesive and elegant than my current ideas. For example, what the hell is even occurring with the two poems I wrote at the lake (12/24/25)? I mean I understand it took much thought, and the themes are pretty core to who I am, but poems are for pussies. Ooh alliteration?

There must be a correlation between time without distraction -> more thinking -> more depression.

I do have many non-depressing topics that I would LOVE to write about, but I’m currently a liiiitttle busy. Putting it lightly. Did you know in 20 days, my life will reach the singular day I’ve been dreaming of; the culmination of my desires beginning in November 2024. A volcanic eruption. 

I’m also concerned with my lack of motivation, or fire, for this competition. Have I subconsciously given up? What is wrong with me? I feel so utterly out-of-sync with myself. I don’t know how I’m feeling at all. It’s as if each day I’m just some viewer orb in a skin suit, trying to stay afloat, so that my owner can come replace me eventually. Obviously I should be studying and learning for the fun of it. I mean these problems are actually insanely cool, so at least that gives me an “out” as to why I’m not too focused on the competition. 

What do I want? Is there legitimately anything in my life that I’ve put the work into and reaped the rewards? Did I sacrifice enough to be able to do well in this competition? 

Is it normal for someone to feel this internal conflict? Is this stress? Anxiety? For some reason, I never am able to tell when I’m feeling that way. I’m sure its underlying effects impact my mindset and daily life, but I cannot pinpoint specifically if I feel stressed or not. Recently, I’ve been dreaming wild scenarios, once even dealing WITH the competition. Maybe that’s a sign?

I think it’s best if I just let these feelings be for now, or “ride the wave,” or go with the flow. Because what else is there to do if not to push on. If not to stay optimistic. If not to crush everyone. If not to work my absolute hardest and drag myself to my limits. 

Because how else can I be myself?

… 

I guess I just want to look back on today, on all my preparation, and feel proud of myself. Even if I fail. 

And even if I fail. I hope I can forgive myself. For not trying harder. For wasting time. For the disappointment.

Signed
