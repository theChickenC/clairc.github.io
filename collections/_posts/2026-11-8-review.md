**ManHwa Review**: Superpowered insect-action fantasy

The concept of the hive and insect-people is so interesting. My favourite arc was the tarantula complex guy (with the kiss of death I think) mainly because it was a fresh perspective didn't involve the MC. I also love the side stories that are still releasing. However, in the later seasons, the story starts to feel repetitive: villains attack, the MC gets stronger, and he beats up some kids. For so much focus on him and the love interest, their personalities are the stereotypical gritty OP protaganists. Overall, it's a heart-warming, concise read, and I'm really glad I gave it a shot. 

**Rating**: B Tier
