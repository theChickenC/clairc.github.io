**Movie Review**: Japanese high-school teacher-student romance

The MC is a girl who, I believe, lost her parents and is kind of autistic. She’s struggling with learning English despite excelling at other subjects, which is why the school pushes her to take extra English lessons with the hottest teacher in the school. The girls are jealous of her and pick on her because she doesn't show emotions and despises crying. But eventually, with this fine ahh teacher, she releases her anger and sadness, which is really cute. I don't exactly remember what happens next though–I may have skimmed the movie. But she has to move, but before she does, they reunite and kiss!

Personally, I believe that it's a really cute story. However, my method of consumption was a really low quality YouTube video, I hope to rewatch it sometime. The vibes of a 2010s Japanese movie is seriously unparalleled, and the actors are so good looking!

**Rating**: A Tier
