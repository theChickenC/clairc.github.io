**Book Review**: post-apocalyptic techno-horror

**My Thoughts**: Uh. I don't know if I didn't let this soak in enough, but it wasn't that crazy as everyone mentioned it to be. Yes, eternal torture is terrifying, and not being able to delete your own existence is horrible, but I feel like I need more concrete examples of what AM does to them (and once they die, how the main character is coping internally). 

Seems to me, the main character is fine and almost happy he got one over on AM in the end. Plus, they have to journey through harsh terrain on AM bodily secretions for what? I don't get why you can't just stop, as the main character legit says he'll be fed eventually, since AM gotta keep them alive. 

And why is the introduction so unrelated? I was so confused. Still highly recommend though.

**Rating**: C Tier
