**Movie Review**: American dystopian romantic comedy

**Before reading reviews**:
MANNN I wish this plot would be about fighting the tech company that installed the surveillance. I’m telling you: what if they broke into the company, disabled all marks, and THEN was able to “advance the plot.” They casted well though; main character was so pretty. This was a refreshing movie, and I’m glad I watched it.

**After reading reviews**: 
Other people online agree with me(!): “It almost feels like it's building to something to show that this system will fall - and the movie ends with a one year time skip which feels tailor made to show this...but it's not! The film ends still in this [messed] up world after drawing so much attention to how it's [messed] up!”


**Rating**: B Tier

**Potential Biases**: I was multitasking while watching this movie, and also I didn't completely finish it to be honest, maybe ending 20 minutes or so earlier.
