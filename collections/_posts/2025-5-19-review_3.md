**Movie Review**: American animated fantasy comedy film 

**My Thoughts**: I heard about this movie many times, but I never watched it. However, I'm glad I eventually did because it is incredibly cute, as it touches on major topics (depression + existentialism) in a light way. Though, I forget exactly what about them. 

I will say that the ending was stupid. Stupid in a way that makes me laugh. It turned into a story inception through 3 generations. Honestly, I sort of don't relate to the characters, except when Orion says "I'll do it afraid." I really agree with that. I'm actually going to note that on my affirmations RIGHT NOW. LOL. ALSO, I loved the helpers of Dark, so cute.

**Rating**: C Tier
