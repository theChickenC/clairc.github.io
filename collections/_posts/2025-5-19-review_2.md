**Show Review**: American legal drama thriller

**My Thoughts**: This show is my crack. There is so much drama, romance and thriller. I just love how the Keating circle slowly grows and grows. I think Oliver just exists as a plot hole coverup, (or someone to get the plot moving) idk the name. He just hacks in 0.3 secs and gets them evidence to win the case. I'm not complaining I just find it silly. I also think Asher would be SO CUTE if he just lost a little weight. The show depicts black and poor empowerment without making it obvious or repungent. This show is just a staple that everyone needs to watch at least once.

**Rating**: A Tier
