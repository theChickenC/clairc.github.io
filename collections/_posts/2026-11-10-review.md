**ManHwa Review**: Dystopian superpowered action fantasy

WOWOWOW. I'm really into this MC, Sayeon. She’s extremely engaging and promising, almost carrying the whole story right now. In my opinion, I wish there was more development of the other characters, especially White. We know some details, but it's not enough. Everyone else has narrated parts of an episode except for him I swear. For one, we know he doesn't even like Sayeon because Red’s affection toward her makes him jelly.

Overall, the idea of aberrants is super interesting, and the ability to borrow powers from deities. The story as a whole is exciting, but I feel like some arcs start to drag. For example, the most recent arc where they're fighting the old female sea wolf (Lee’s mom’s doomed yuri) feels a billion chapters long. 

Unfortunately, they're on hiatus right now, but coming back soon–excited!

**Rating**: B Tier
