If April is the cruellest month, and<br>
I think about the future and murder<br>
The present, then, this is how I live.<br>

Blackening spring walnuts<br>
Fuzzy ~~black~~ ~~amber~~ sepia caterpillars<br>
Crammed into my jar.<br>
Honeyed rain masks my the scent of longing<br>
Suffocating putrid.<br>
Dearest signs of entropy unravel in bleeding tweets<br>
Memories of old and future purposely drown current [s]<br>
Life tortures; for why must I<br>
Lose feeling to achieve bliss<br>
My existence is paradox.<br>
If a bud could mourn its<br>
Sprout and pray for its bloom.<br>

**Side Note**: Inspired by "The Waste Land", a 1922 long poem by T.S. Eliot.

**6 Hours Later**: I am ahead—I just read the following quote from The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success” by Deepak Chopra.<br>

“The past, present, and future are all properties of consciousness. The past is recollection, memory; the future is anticipation; the present is awareness. Therefore time is the movement of thought. Both past and future are born in the imagination; only the present, which is awareness, is real and eternal. It is. It is the potentiality of space-time, matter, and energy. It is an eternal field of possibilities experiencing itself as abstract forces, whether they be light, heat, electricity, magnetism, or gravity. These forces are neither in the past nor in the future. They just are.”
