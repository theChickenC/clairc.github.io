**Book Review**: YA cyberpunk novel

It's funny. I was worried I wasn't gonna finish this book, but actually I did. 

I thought the MC was self-contradicting at times, but overall this book was well written. I liked that spice was hinted at, but wasn't able to be followed through on, which makes perfect sense for the setting and amount of angst in this book. 

To be honest, the ending surprised me; I thought it was gonna be a typical one where the main character wins, but I guess the author got lazy, because it's left on a positive note, but still unfinished. But also, they built up the lab entrance and break in so much, only for it to be absolutely mid. Like it ended in 2 pages? I did like the moral though, don't count on someone else (Lark) to save you. Only you can save yourself. I also felt like the rest of the group were barely fleshed out... maybe in the sequel. 

Kind of a bittersweet, sad-ish read, but very fresh, and loved the Asian American representation. Also loved the cyberpunk genre and book cover.

**Rating**: B Tier
