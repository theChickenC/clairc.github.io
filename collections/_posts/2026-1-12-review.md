**Book Review**: a YA cyberpunk novel

It's funny. I was worried I wasn't gonna finish this book, but actually I did. I thought the MC was self-contradicting at times, but overall well written. I liked that spice was hinted at, but wasn't able to be followed through on, which makes perfect sense for the setting & amount of angst in this book. Tbh, the ending surprised me; I thought it was gonna be a typical MC wins, but is the author got lazy, like it's left on a positive note, but still unfinished.

But also, they built up the lab entrance/break in so much, only for it to be absolutely mild. Like it ended in 2 pages? and I did like the moral though, don't count on someone else (Lark) to save you. Only you can save yourself. I also felt like the rest of the group were barely fleshed out... maybe in the sequel. Kind of a bittersweet, sad ish read, but very fresh, and loved the AA rep. Also loved the cyberpunk + cover.
