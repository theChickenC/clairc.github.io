**Movie Review**: American heist action adventure

**My Thoughts**: THIS. This is what I want in a movie: adventure, comedy, action, love, mystery, fantasy. These genres are the literal epitome of how movies are supposed to make you feel. Encapsulating an other world for you to experience. There were beautiful scenes, especially inside the pyramid, library, and intro in Thailand. Speaking of Thailand, the action scene in the beginning was so good. I haven't seen one like that in a WHILE. Loved how antagonist died from his own greed at the end. Rightfully so. Overall, great PG option!

**Rating**: A Tier
