**Movie Review**: American animated musical fantasy

**My Thoughts**: LOVE. Love the music, art, comedy. One of the best movies of this year. Not only is it refreshing with Asian characters and plot, it is also wonderfully executed in terms of animation and plot. No wonder this movie is blowing up. Also, the themes are strong too: self-confidence, family, goodness. I LOVE the one Dr. scene, when he makes the faces at the girls (T-T), legit laughing so hard. I also like the fact they didn't start romance between Rumi and Jinu. He is like 900 years old, and Rumi is maybe 20. Good job writers. I hope you get a billion dollars salary.

**Rating**: A Tier
