**Book Review**: Dystopian war-thriller

Yep, I get it. It's portraying our  humanity and how even journalism can become an immoral act. I think if there was more background on why the Civil War broke out in the first place, rather than a focus on thematic concepts of the effects of war, it’d be more appealing to me. This movie is highly unique, but obviously spreads a message. I think <a href="/temp" target="_blank">Don’t Look Up</a> has better execution because the entertainment factor masks the message more.

**Rating**: B Tier

**Potential Biases**: Someone spoke highly of this film, so I was influenced to watch it.
