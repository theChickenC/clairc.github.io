**Movie Review**: epic science fiction

**My Thoughts**: Gorgeous and pleasing to watch. It made me wish for fantasy worlds, but at the same time appreciate places on Earth, like 张家界. The aesthetic beauty only contributes to the mature themes of trust, courage, and nature. I can see why it shocked the world in 2009, as it moves me even now. To be honest, I didn't watch it fully to the end. That's not because of the movie though, it's because I was sleepy. Now, I want to watch Avatar 2 lol.

**Rating**: B Tier
