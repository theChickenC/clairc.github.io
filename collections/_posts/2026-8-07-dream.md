**Setting**: Okay lol. So we're at a new place completely—sorta like the hotel I stayed at for Miles' Michigan stuff. It's highly populated, and lots of people my age roam about in the lounge or entrance. The funny part is that there's also a college building connected to it with a library (like CMU's Huntsman), aquarium, a literal beach (?) (like a sunbathing + swimming area made from artificial rock/terrain, no sand though), and lecture hall. So on each floor, there's a soft serve dispenser with 3 different flavors with toppings that change everyday. On each floor, the elevator will ask you a math-based riddle, giving you a set of clues to access the next floor. 

**Events**: Because I wanted ice cream so bad, I'd get ice cream at least 4x through this system. There was also stuff I had to do during the day, but I can't remember what exactly. Definitely something about taking a class with a teacher setting, but unconventional tasks. I also remember stressing about not having any summer programs—and I remember Washington & Lee being mentioned. Supposedly, my brother & I were the only ones there, but mom's voice told me that lol.
