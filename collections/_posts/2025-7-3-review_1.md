**Book Review**: YA contemporary paranormal fiction

**My Thoughts**: This book was a mix of realistic fiction, dystopia, romance. I thought it was wonderfully executed until the ending. First of all, Mari's trauma with her mom and their relationship is depicted perfectly. I completely understood and empathized with it. Then, the whole afterlife system was super new and interesting. I would've liked explanation of the tech tho. How does it work? How was it created in the first place? These questions lay unanswered. Now, the ending. It felt rushed, especially after Mari is on trial. How does she even go free? It seems that the Powers That Be aren't super dictatorial. 

**Rating**: C Tier
