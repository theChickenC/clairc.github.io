**Book Review**: YA queer mystery thriller

**My Thoughts**: SO Comedic and realistic. It's what all those weird ahh highschool murder mysteries want to be, without the annoying usage of Ivy leagues and college pressures. I love that the MC has relatable character traits, like observational skills in all facets. If it were me, I'd def be into Benji.

It's so cool that MC does martial arts, is into cute stuff, and has nerdy friends! Even though there's lots of mentions of sexuality (MC is gay), it's not that overbearing, considering the author uses IBS for the plot about the same frequency. I personally love that it wasn't too dark, but still a complex story; there's mention of poverty struggles. I feel like the author spent SO much time on this book because of its quality and wordplay mastery.

**Rating**: A Tier
