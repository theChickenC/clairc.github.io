**Book Review**: YA dark fantasy

I really like the idea of the Players and how a special gold can ward them off. I also love how the MC had to unlearn habits that arose from always having to tell the truth, and I wish that was developed more. The requited romance kinda shocked me, I didn’t think Player Jude would reciprocate her feelings. To be honest, I think I read up until MC threatened Jude, and they left the Theater together. Then I read the ending about them killing Sil (which makes sense, it is obvious he is the primary instigator). The world-building is super cool: the Reverers, Players letting magic take over them rather than act, etc. I would have loved to learned more about it.

**Rating**: D Tier

**Potential Biases**: I did not finish reading it; I discovered the plot twist through reviews online afterward.
