Action-packed dream, and the vibes and the tension was nice.

**Setting**: I was on a mission, starting off on a crowded public transportation (bus or subway). It was super stressful because strange people were coming on, but Mom was there too. 

**Events**: Eventually, I tried move to a spot near the front because I wanted to see out the front, and someone was blocking my view. However, there were 2 girls in the seat already, and they purposely took up more space to push me off into the aisle, despite there being enough room. That's why I found my own empty seat (fully empty). When people asked to sit with me, I screamed "NO!" and yelled "those b's didn't let me sit with them etc etc!" Anyways, Mom told me to stop causing a scene. Then, I met a guy and some female friend my age, but they turn out to be on the dark side. There was some purple faceless creatures that they needed to meet with, and I obviously wasn't allowed to go with, so I stayed behind and found a slightly crazy middle-aged man. Asking for his help and in return helping him rescue his daughter, we trailed the two. They (the secretive duo) were teaming with the museum employees. We (man and I) had to hide from them behind a wall because we snuck in, it was seriously high-stakes if they caught us, or at least that's how I felt in the moment.
