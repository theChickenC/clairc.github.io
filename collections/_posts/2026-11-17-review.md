**ManHwa Review**: Adult sci-fi political thriller

I'm not finished as of right now, but It's really good so far.

I love the idea of a floating city that not only shields citizens, but is also constantly moving. And I'm also interested in, obviously, the murder of Martha, but what are the chances that Livia is working for Tully? She'll be able to bail the team out of anything, and they’re able to have a first-hand connection to the current political turmoil. Is Tully actually popular enough to be asked for a job from a gazillionaire? Martha could situate her sister literally anywhere else. Oh yeah, I forgot about the anonymous informant, what happened to him?

Overall, this book is really well written and tidy–I particularly love the sci-fi aspect of the world. I can sense the “adult” genre simply based on the writing style; it's just so mature and almost lacking an element of imagination. Sure, there’s high-tech, AI, and climate change, but the emphasis on journalism and politics skews it to be realistic fiction. Still, it's really good.

**Rating**: C Tier
