**Show Review**: supernatural mystery comedy

**My Thoughts**: This was so good; I essentially binged it. Everyone was insanely cute and gorgeous including the new invisible girl, the zombie, and even the Hyde 😅. Nom nom nom. 

I think that whole plot of Enid & Wednesday switching bodies was silly, but that was only a 1 or a few episodes. 

Speaking of Enid, GIRL why would you break up with that beautiful medusa man. He is so good looking it's crazy, even with a beanie. People hate on this show series, but I don't get why, it's so good for escapism and easily relatable for high school kids. 

**Rating**: A Tier
