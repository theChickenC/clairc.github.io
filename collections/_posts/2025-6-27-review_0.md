**Movie Review**: American spy action thriller

**My Thoughts**: The message was nice: not everyone is inherently a killer; you can outwit others if you are worse in other aspects. It's an action movie dealing with the CIA, and a super smart MC. Have to say, I was VERY satisfied when the MC tricked everyone, even though it's very obviously plot armour. I can also appreciate the fresh new perspectives + scenes. It was fun to watch with Miles.

**Rating**: C Tier
