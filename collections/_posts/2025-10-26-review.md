**Book Review**: YA supernatural horror

**My Thoughts**: Holy cow. I didn't expect this book to be so gory in its descriptions, but I enjoyed it. It crept me out with all the bugs, rotting, and masks with faces. It was a great twist for her skills gained from struggling with bipolar disorder—to move a finger at a time—to be the action that saves everyone at the end. I thought Flynn was fr going to die; so glad he didn't (I would've tweaked ngl). I hope they all heal! This book is perf for Halloween & is smth I'd love to discuss with a friend, if they would be willing to 🥹

**Rating**: A Tier
