**A couple things**:

When you look back from, let's say a yr, won't all your goals/worries be meaningless?

How do we decide to live a meaningful life? I can imagine the different possibilities of my future, and each one feels so weird to me.

Why does time move so weirdly? Imagine your father at your age, and now he's like 40. He's lived an entire life, while I've only been here for a bit. But we both coexist & experience time together.

And right now, you experience the present. But literally your present is the future too. It feels as if each moment is simultaneous.

Isn't better to be free from those questions above rather than actually contemplating them? Like when you're busy w/ school or material problems in life, it's so much easier to deal with. But as much as these thoughts are dangerous, they're also so tempting to think about.

Do I enjoy making myself sad/contemplative? Rainy nights where I stare outside & question life is appealing to me sometimes. But I know I shouldn't do that. 1) lazy 2) dangerous.

I used to say my dream was to live a happy life, but the great fallacy is that humans will never be fully content, so does it even matter? Do I start basing worth off of pure quantitative achievement? Or go w/ no plan in life? Just live in the present?

Maybe.
