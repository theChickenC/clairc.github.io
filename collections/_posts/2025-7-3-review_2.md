**Book Review**: YA dark fantasy

**My Thoughts**: I loved this writing style and setting. Everything was so gorgeous, dark academia, and fantasy-filled. Just amazing. I wish I could watch a movie of it, yk? 

I actually did not finish the book because I was reading it for ~2 hrs only (extenuating circumstance), but I did like what I saw. However, online people are complaining about the MC's personality (stubborn & stupid) and the pacing. I agree with the personality aspect, MC was look annoying. Can't speak to the pacing aspect, I don't know lol. I would like a chance to finish in the future; it's def the vibes that I dream about (ifykyk) (I am the only one that knows)

**Side Note** At the time of uploading this online, I have no idea what type of vibes. Like I have a guess, obviously that otherwordly type vibe, but that's not significant nor well-known enough for me to hint at it like that 💀

**Rating**: B Tier
