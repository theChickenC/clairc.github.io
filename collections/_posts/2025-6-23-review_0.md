**Movie Review**: South Korean supernatural horror

**My Thoughts**: I really enjoyed watching this because shaman type horror is rare. Also, I loved that it was a happy ending. I didn't really get the cultural nuances at first, but once looking it up it became clear this movie is a Mona Lisa. Seriously. 

Bong-gil, the young guy, was SOOO cute, especially because of the man bun and scripture! Nom. Nom.

Overall, would recommend this to anyone able to watch with Eng sub. Normally, if I weren't on a plane, I wouldn't have been able to watch this. Just wish the movie was longer. I think this could be turned into a series.

**Rating**: A Tier
