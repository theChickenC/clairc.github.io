**Show Review**: American psychological thriller

**My Thoughts**: This entranced me at first because the script was so unique. You hardly get shows with this level of dialogue and creativity. Honestly, the should've picked a different looking actor LOL (for Joe). But, he did play the role of a sociopathic serial killer pretty well. Not too sure how I feel about the later seasons, but the S1 was SO good.

**Rating**: A Tier

**Potential Biases**: I stopped watching after S3, and I skipped S2.
