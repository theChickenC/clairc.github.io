**Book Review**: YA Taiwanese American treasure-hunt rom-com

Holy cow. It was so unexpected that I went onto Goodreads to read the reviews to make sure I wasn’t going crazy. The cover is a TAD misleading because I thought the book would be light and fun. No, it’s  puzzle upon puzzle upon puzzle.

The cover image is so SCRUMPTIOUS. Seriously, I want to recreate it and wear a cute striped tank and cargo pants of my own. But it doesn't match the MC’s personality at all. I promise you, in real life she does not look like that–if she did, her personality would be way more confident.

There’s a farrago of events going on all at once: glimpses of Taiwan, Gemma’s romantic relationships, and little fun facts about her family’s past. However, this caused it to be genuinely bizarre to read, as I’d flip a page and a new topic would present without a connection between these disparate ideas. 

I feel like I wasn’t able to grasp the underlying motivation for the plot. For example, Gemma’s super insecure about not knowing Chinese, and ever since she arrived in Taiwan, she's like, "I don't know any of these words, but everyone else knows. I’m such a chud loser." And then she randomly eurekas, "Oh, not everyone else can speak Chinese. Why did I think I was the dumbest one here and assume that?" The internal conflict has no backstory or grounding, and the resolution is just her going, "I'm so silly, why was I insecure in the first place?"

Okay, I don't know how big Taiwan is, but the timeline doesn't make sense. Tell me how you're visiting every single tourist attraction under the sun, traveling throughout Taiwan, in two weeks.

What rating would I give it? 

**Rating**: F tier, because I was disappointed. If I wasn't entranced by the cover, I would've given it a D tier.
