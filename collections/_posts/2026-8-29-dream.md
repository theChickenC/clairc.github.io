Cool dream about foraging & friends, potentially a world idea.

**Setting**: Most of my close friends were seen in this dream. Basically, we're going through our school schedules, practicing the route for Day 1. But like... the dream is a hybrid of doing that while simultaneously going around in people's lawns and neighborhood forest.

**Events**: One of my friends' neighborhood had so many FRUITS on the trees. So tangerines were on the trees, but only 1 per tree in the most difficult to access spots. Winans comes in talking to a resident of the community, explaining a tool to help him obtain the fruit. However, I just climb it, and reach it. Later, Pearl has a moral dilemma—urging me to return the fruit to the man. I guess I listen because I don't live here—she does—and we were just heading to her house to grab something. Then, on the hill, I see UNPEELED tangerines in the dirt (in the dream they are a real fruit (a type of growing method, like a potato)). Anyways, I collected all of them & was planning to wash the dirt off to eat.

**Theory**: I think I was inspired by the Ssundee Blood & Bones Series I was rewatching.
