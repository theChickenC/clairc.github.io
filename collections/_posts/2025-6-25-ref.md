I want to be a fictional type of beautiful. This would only be possible in places I’ve daydreamed about. I want to go to a boarding school where dark magic is prevalent. The sky is permanently dusk, and cool, foggy clouds rain often. With an aura that radiates otherworldly. Skin as fair as an unblemished egg, with deep red lips I already own. Hair that resembles a void. My body thin but with muscles that ripple through me when I flow.  

Exactly 171.000 cm. Legs as long as a giraffe's neck. But I also have a giraffe's neck. 

I wrote that first part a year ago, and now I can’t help but make this a satire. You want to waste your (mental) energy and (mental) health on constantly trying to be “the best?” Comparing yourself to others, changing yourself, being irritated at yourself? That’s stupid. So stop. If I have any tips I’ll let you know!
