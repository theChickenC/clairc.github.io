Walking around in circles, in and out of regal glass buildings, going through heated tunnels, and back outside facing the stinging breeze, we grow upset. Then, as we walking through into another building, I see some familiar summer program faces. They're also tense I think? People also gather in a large circle—doing some activity like a group test they have to pass. Later, I check and it's BEDWARS??!?! Like you have to win a match. Also for some reason when I talked to someone, I turned into a Minecraft skin, so I crouched twice to grab her attention.
