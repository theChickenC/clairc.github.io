## APT
* I think it is a really catchy song, which makes it perfect for Just Dance. 
* The dance moves, despite being easy, are still ubiquitous, and I feel like I’d be able to use them in other situations. 
* It’s not too athletic/a workout dance, while still being continuously engaging.   
* **Rating**: A Tier 

## Cry Baby
* Wow! This one sure is difficult. Defintely a slower, more flowy style of dancing.
* **Rating**: C Tier
 
## Kitipo
* This song is so funny. This dance would be fun to do every once in a while! The movements are unique.
* **Rating**: D Tier

## Viva La Vida
* SILLY DANCE. Obviously an amazing slow song, I love playing this one with my brother.
* **Rating**: C Tier

## Counting Stars
* Wow, I’m surprised they chose this dancing style for this song. It’s more of a softer lyrical dance rather than hip hop. Again, love playing this one with my brother.
* **Rating**: B Tier

## Thrift Shop
* I’m a sucker for any fast-paced hiphop. However, I was surprised by the amount of rap in the song lol.
* **Rating**: B Tier
