**Book Review**: YA fantasy dystopian

**My Thoughts**: Hands down best book I've ever read. The setting is so magical and steam punk vibes—just envision a set of floating islands with metal animals and flying ships. Straight gas. 

The main character is so lovable, fierce, unrelenting, compassionate, and he's better than everyone—always wins. He's so smart and can catch snakes FAST. I didn't pay much attention to the dystopia; I am more captivated by the refreshing, novel setting and magic system. Also, imagine battling an 800 ft snake. Like 😮. 

This book will haunt me because I'll spend the rest of my life looking for something like it. It's rare for high fantasy to be romance free & true action. But I LOVE it. You are utterly transported into Conrad's world, in a way most books fail to do so. So happy I found this.

**Rating**: S Tier
