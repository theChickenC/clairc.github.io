**Movie Review**: American supernatural psychological horror

**My Thoughts**: This was a classic. I think I already watched some of the beginning before. But definitely not the ending. I thought it was great to watch with friends but no way in heck am I watching it by myself. I mean there's not much to say about the movie, however the memory I have with Jason, Sophia, Josh, and others is irreplaceable.

**Rating**: B Tier
