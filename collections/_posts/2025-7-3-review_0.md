**Book Review**: YA true crime mystery

**My Thoughts**: I honestly love this book. It wasn't too deep, easy to read, and has a happy ending. I like that the MC wasn't obnoxious; she was smart! But because it's so simple, there's really not much to say. There are no themes that stand out, or beautiful descriptions that haunt me. This is a good book to ease back into reading, after not doing so for essentially a whole school year... Lol. 

Also, there are some plot holes. Why did Willow's mom remarry w/ the plastic surgeon? Overall, nice book, pretty tame but also enthralling!

**Rating**: B Tier
