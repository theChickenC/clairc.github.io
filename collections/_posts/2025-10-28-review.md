**Book Review**: YA contemporary fantasy

**My Thoughts**: I absolutely love that Sel is a main love interest. I mean I knew it based on their interactions & the way the author delineated it, but still yes.

It also talks a lot about death & grief, which adds another dimension to the book. And it's so unexpected that Bree has all 3 magic: Arthur, Vera, Medium? Author also depicted the 'good ole' boys dynamics insanely well, and honestly more books need to incorporate it. 

**Rating**: A Tier

**Side Note**: Unfortunately, I DNF'd the series because it was becoming dense.
