**ManHwa Review**: Mature psychological dark romance

At first I was like, what is this girl doing to this poor innocent man? Why are you tormenting him? But turns out, no, he's actually the mastermind behind all of this. It’s so mind boggling to me that he’s attractive to the extent that people literally want to drug him–the botanist wants to have him as well. 

And tell me why the mom of the male lead drugged him too? What was the purpose? There is no clear lesson that you’re teaching? And lowkey, I feel like the mom knows what's going on between the two of them, because why does she keep insinuating that they have to “get along” with each other for the “ideal family”?

I really enjoy seeing a darker darker side of the ML during season two. The fact that he's a willing participant alleviates some concern. Bro is right where he wants to be. It's a pretty interesting read with good pacing. I also like the childhood friend backstory including the power dynamic. I don't quite understand what's going on with the plot, but let’s be honest, no one's here for the plot anyways.

The MC is still unknown to me. I don't understand her motives or why she even likes the second male lead in the first place.

I will continue reading this. It's better than a lot of other slop out there, and the art is stunning.

**Rating**: A Tier
