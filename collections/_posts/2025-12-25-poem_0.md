Winter does not hurt, it 
Recognizes me. Overpowering
darkness insulates.

When spring comes I am laid bare.
Away from my winter solstice 
1221 is a palindrome,
	12 and 21 not just divisible by 3 but
	Add up to 3.

Tell me I’m lucky my Fate is destined Tell me or else I won’t have hope Tell me or I won’t live to remember

Gold cannot be
	With the dainty flowers and succulent clovers width of a penny

Only appreciated in the summer, traded in the fall, and forged in the winter.
