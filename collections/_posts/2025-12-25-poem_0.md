Winter does not hurt, it<br>
Recognizes me. Overpowering<br>
darkness insulates.<br>

When spring comes I am laid bare.<br>
Away from my winter solstice<br>
1221 is a palindrome,<br>
&emsp;12 and 21 not just divisible by 3 but<br>
&emsp;Add up to 3.<br>

Tell me I’m lucky my Fate is destined Tell me or else I won’t have hope Tell me or I won’t live to remember<br>

Gold cannot be<br>
&emsp;With the dainty flowers and succulent clovers width of a penny<br>

Only appreciated in the summer, traded in the fall, and forged in the winter.<br>
