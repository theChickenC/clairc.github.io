DigiHand

Turning Chicken Scratch to Text

DigiHand, an open-source graphical user interface software that converts scanned pages of handwriting into editable text, written in Python. I built a side-by-side editor with synchronized scrolling to make the review process effortless, and used AI models for transcription. Using DigiHand, I easily converted my journals into e-books, and other people with the same needs can now accelerate their digitization process too.

There is no inexpensive, user-friendly tool available to digitize handwriting. Both authors and the everyday person have artifacts they want to preserve and edit, but they may not write software. Recognizing the high demand for a solution, I made DigiHand open-source.
